Kevin Schoenholzer, PhD

I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Communication and Public Policy, University of Lugano (Università della Svizzera italiana, USI) Switzerland, in the research group of Prof. Dr. Benedetto Lepori.
My research examines social stratification through quantitative analysis, modeling institutional, individual, and regional influences, and comparing how educational systems shape life trajectories across populations.

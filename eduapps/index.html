Research software

lsastrat: stratification metrics for large-scale assessments

An R package for the quantities that research on educational inequality reports from PISA, TIMSS, PIRLS and PIAAC: socio-economic achievement gradients, school segregation indices and the Ferreira-Gignoux inequality of educational opportunity. It pools plausible values and uses the replicate weights of each survey for the standard errors. Further functions decompose these quantities, compare them across countries and cycles, and run specification-curve analyses. On the official PISA 2022 data, the estimates agree with the intsvy and segregation packages.

Teaching tools

Educational Stratification in PISA

A browser tool for teaching the analysis of educational inequality with PISA student data from 101 countries and eight cycles, 2000 to 2022. Students estimate socio-economic gradients, achievement gaps, variance decompositions and within-country trends. The result panels show the R code that reproduces each estimate, and every estimator is checked against R.

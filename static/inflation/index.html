Inflationary biases (aggregate > 0)

  • Money illusion & anchoring: slow wage/price adjustment; nominal anchors lag.
  • Contract stickiness: loans, rents, salaries need renegotiation to preserve real value.
  • Spend-now bias: higher opportunity cost of cash encourages consumption.

Deflationary effects (aggregate < 0)

  • Intertemporal substitution: defers purchases; saving looks better.
  • Debt burden rises in real terms; fixed payments weigh more.
  • Sources: benign productivity/efficiency vs. bad deflation from deleveraging after over-leverage (e.g., 1920).
